## Lab 8 — Neural Networks with PyTorch

- **Aim:** Build and train neural networks using PyTorch and a custom training loop.
- **Main tasks:**
  - Explore tensors and verify automatic gradients numerically.
  - Build a neural network using `nn.Module`.
  - Implement training and evaluation functions.
  - Use `DataLoader` for mini-batching and shuffling.
  - Apply dropout and weight decay.
  - Compare training and validation performance.
- **Tools:** Python, PyTorch, NumPy, and Matplotlib.
