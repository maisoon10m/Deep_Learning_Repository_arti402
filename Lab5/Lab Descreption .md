## Lab 5 — Optimization Algorithms

- **Aim:** Understand how optimizers affect neural network training.
- **Main tasks:**
  - Implement backpropagation and verify gradients numerically.
  - Implement SGD, Momentum, RMSProp, and Adam from scratch.
  - Explore the effects of batch size and learning rate.
  - Compare training and test performance on a three-class spiral dataset.
- **Tools:** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, and TensorFlow Playground.
